# Palette's UX Journal

## 2025-03-05 - [Keyboard Navigation and Shortcuts in cashier POS systems]
**Learning:** Cashiers in high-throughput retail environments rely heavily on standard keyboard shortcuts (F2 to search, F8/F9 to checkout, and Escape to dismiss modals) rather than manual mouse navigation. Integrating these keys dramatically increases checkout speed and reduces cognitive load, while visual `<kbd>` badges enhance discovery and overall screen-reader/accessibility.
**Action:** Always provide standard, conflict-free keyboard shortcuts for intensive data-entry or transactional screens, paired with visually aligned `<kbd>` badges to ensure clear and actionable user-experience indicators.
