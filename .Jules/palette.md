## 2026-03-05 - Confirmation of Destructive Actions & Accessible Cart Controls in POS
**Learning:** Bulk actions in POS (Point of Sale) modules, such as emptying the entire shopping cart, are critical touchpoints. Executing them without confirmation can cause severe disruption to cashiers during busy hours due to accidental clicks. Additionally, standard icon-only buttons (like quantity incrementers, decrements, or single removals) must always have descriptive ARIA labels and clear focus states to ensure keyboard navigation and screen reader accessibility.
**Action:** Always wrap bulk destructive operations (like cart clearing) in confirmation dialogs (e.g. `window.confirm` or styled overlays). Furthermore, equip all icon-only buttons with explicit `title` and `aria-label` attributes alongside `focus-visible` styling patterns.
